# Case Study | Product Iteration & UX Leadership

[English](../../README.md) | [日本語](../../README.ja.md)

## Building a better end-to-end experience

A product is defined not only by its feature list but by how naturally those features work together.

Ekiyomi evolved through iterative improvements to first-time onboarding, voice controls, continuous playback, and brand consistency—all designed around the experience of young train enthusiasts.

## 01 | First-time onboarding

**Opportunity:** Help new users understand line selection, station playback, and next-stop navigation quickly.

**Improvement:** Introduced a step-by-step onboarding experience and a way to revisit usage guidance from the settings screen.

**Capability demonstrated:** UX Definition · Onboarding Strategy · Feature Delivery

## 02 | Voice selection & continuous play

**Opportunity:** Make it easier to choose a voice and enjoy a sequence of stations.

**Improvement:** Simplified voice options, enabled sample playback during onboarding, and added continuous next-stop playback with clear stop conditions when the user changes stations, direction, or screen.

**Capability demonstrated:** User Journey Design · Interaction Design · Requirements Prioritization

## 03 | Product identity across platforms

**Opportunity:** Give Ekiyomi a recognizable, cohesive identity from app icon to shared-link preview.

**Improvement:** Selected and deployed an icon concept featuring a train with a speech bubble, with consistent presentation across iOS, Web, and link previews.

**Capability demonstrated:** Product Positioning · Design Decisions · Cross-Platform Consistency

## Product leadership across the cycle

- **Define:** connect each improvement to a user experience objective.
- **Prioritize:** select enhancements based on their effect on the overall product.
- **Orchestrate:** use AI-supported implementation to deliver iterations.
- **Review:** evaluate the resulting functionality and UX.
- **Deliver:** maintain a coherent experience across Web and iOS.

## What this demonstrates

**Feature execution is only part of delivery.** The larger capability is to set a product direction, coordinate implementation, make trade-offs, and continuously improve the user journey.

**Capabilities:** Product Ownership · UX Leadership · Feature Prioritization · AI-Assisted Execution · Cross-Platform Delivery
